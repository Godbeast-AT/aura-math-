01

Overview

Aura Math is a privacy-first calculator app. We built it with a simple principle: a calculator has no business knowing who you are, where you are, or what you're calculating.

This policy explains exactly what data exists when you use Aura Math, where it lives, and what we do with it (spoiler: nothing, because it never leaves your phone).

Short version: Aura Math stores your preferences and calculation history locally on your device only. We do not collect personal data. We do not have servers storing your information. We do not sell or share anything about you.

02

Data We Collect

Aura Math collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-ins, no user profiles, and no tracking identifiers. The only data that exists is what you create yourself, stored locally on your device.

⚙️

App Preferences

Theme choice, button animation settings, clear confirmation toggle — saved locally via your device's storage.

🧮

Calculation History

Your recent calculations, if the history feature is enabled. Stored only on-device and clearable anytime.

🎨

Custom Settings

Any custom themes, saved formulas, or widget configurations you create. Yours, on your device.

03

Local Storage & How It Works

Aura Math uses your device's local storage (via browser localStorage or Capacitor's native storage) to remember your preferences between sessions. This means:

  • All data is stored directly on your device — not on any server.
  • No data is transmitted over the internet as part of normal app usage.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data completely.
  • You can clear app data at any time through your device's settings.
  • Nothing is backed up to cloud services by the app itself.

The data saved locally is minimal: a JSON object containing your selected theme, toggle states for settings, and optionally your recent calculation history. None of this identifies you as a person.

04

What We Don't Collect

To be completely clear, Aura Math does not collect, transmit, or store any of the following:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any contact information
  • Your location or GPS data
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or IMEI numbers
  • IP address or network information
  • Usage analytics or behavioral tracking data
  • Crash reports or diagnostic logs sent to any server
  • Camera, microphone, or contacts data
  • Financial information of any kind

We do not use any advertising SDKs, analytics platforms, or tracking libraries inside the app. No ads. No trackers. No surveillance.

05

App Permissions

Aura Math requests only the permissions it genuinely needs. Below is a full list of what is requested and why:

  • Storage / Local Storage — Required to save your theme and settings preferences between sessions.
  • Internet (future features) — May be requested in future versions for optional live currency exchange rates. This will be opt-in and clearly disclosed when introduced.
  • Camera (future features) — If OCR-based math scanning is added, camera permission will be requested only when you activate that feature.
  • Microphone (future features) — If voice calculator input is added, microphone permission will be requested only when you use it.

Any new permissions required by future features will be disclosed in an updated privacy policy before the feature is released.

06

Third-Party Services

In its current version, Aura Math does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services.

The app is built with React, Vite, and Capacitor — all open-source frameworks that do not collect user data on their own.

If future versions of the app introduce optional third-party integrations (such as live currency rates from an API), those will be clearly documented here, opt-in only, and governed by the respective provider's privacy policy. We will never enable data collection without your knowledge.

Aura Math is distributed via the Google Play Store and/or Apple App Store. Those platforms have their own privacy policies governing app distribution, device analytics, and platform-level data — those policies are separate from this one.

07

Children's Privacy

Aura Math is suitable for users of all ages, including children. Because we collect no personal data and require no account creation, the app is fully compliant with children's privacy laws including COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and GDPR-K provisions.

We do not knowingly collect any data from children under 13, nor do we collect data from any user regardless of age. Parents and guardians can feel confident that installing Aura Math on a child's device poses no privacy risk.

08

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Aura Math grows and new features are added. When we do:

  • The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the revision date.
  • Significant changes — especially those involving new data collection — will be announced via an in-app notice.
  • We will never retroactively change our data practices in a way that is less protective of your privacy without explicit consent.

We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
